批量重铸 / MassReforge

一张券，一次重铸全部适用物品。

- 武器重铸券：重铸背包与快捷栏的武器和工具。
- 饰品重铸券：重铸背包、全部配装、外观与模组饰品槽。
- 词条可自选，武器支持按应用后价值选择“最高级”。
- 至少修改一件才消耗一张券，不额外收费。
- 默认排除灾厄的骇人词条，可在服务端设置中关闭。
- 无需其他模组。

MassReforge

One scroll, one click: every eligible item gets reforged.

- Weapon Reforge Scroll (Goblin Tinkerer, 22 platinum by default): reforges eligible weapons and tools in your inventory and hotbar.
- Accessory Reforge Scroll (Goblin Tinkerer, 35 platinum by default): reforges eligible accessories across your inventory, loadouts, vanity slots, and modded accessory slots.
- Choose from the available modifiers, or use Best Available for weapons to select the modifier with the highest resulting item value. Non-whip summon weapons prefer Ruthless by default.
- Consumes one scroll only if at least one item changes, at no additional cost.
- Calamity's Horrible modifier is excluded by default. Can be disabled in the mod settings.
- No other mods needed.
