[h1]批量重铸 / MassReforge[/h1]
还在为一件件找哥布林工匠重铸发愁？买张重铸券，选好词条，全部适用物品一次搞定。

[list]
[*]武器重铸券（哥布林工匠出售）：覆盖背包与快捷栏的武器和工具。
[*]饰品重铸券（哥布林工匠出售）：覆盖背包、全部配装、外观与模组饰品槽。
[*]词条可自选，支持模组词条，武器支持按应用后价值选择“最高级”。
[*]默认排除灾厄的骇人词条，可在服务端设置中关闭。
[*]至少修改一件才消耗一张券，不额外收取金币。
[*]无需其他模组。
[/list]

[h1]MassReforge[/h1]
Reforge all compatible items with one scroll.

[list]
[*]Weapon Reforge Scroll: sold by the Goblin Tinkerer for 22 platinum. Covers inventory and hotbar weapons, pickaxes, axes and hammers.
[*]Accessory Reforge Scroll: sold for 35 platinum. Covers inventory, all loadouts, vanity and modded accessory slots.
[*]Choose a valid modifier, or use Best Available for weapons. It selects the modifier with the highest resulting item value for each weapon; ties favor the current modifier.
[*]Favorited items are included; cursor items are excluded as targets. Incompatible items and items already matching the target remain unchanged.
[*]Consumes one scroll only when at least one item changes, with no additional coin cost. Preview change, matching and skip counts before confirming.
[*]A server setting excludes Calamity's Horrible modifier by default. It can be disabled and only affects reforge scrolls.
[*]Supports English and Simplified Chinese. No other mods required.
[/list]
