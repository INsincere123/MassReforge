[h1]批量重铸 / MassReforge[/h1]
还在为一件件找哥布林工匠重铸发愁？买张重铸券，选好词条，全部适用物品一次搞定。

[list]
[*]武器重铸券（哥布林工匠出售）：覆盖背包与快捷栏的武器和工具。
[*]饰品重铸券（哥布林工匠出售）：覆盖背包、全部配装、外观与模组饰品槽。
[*]词条可自选，支持模组词条，武器支持按应用后价值选择“最高级”。
[*]非鞭子召唤武器默认优先“无情”，重铸券售价可在设置中调整。
[*]默认排除灾厄的骇人词条，可在服务端设置中关闭。
[*]至少修改一件才消耗，不额外收取金币。
[*]无需其他模组。
[/list]

[h1]MassReforge[/h1]
Tired of running back to the Goblin Tinkerer for every single item? Grab a scroll, pick your modifier, and reforge everything eligible at once.

[list]
[*]Weapon Reforge Scroll (Goblin Tinkerer, 2 platinum by default): reforges eligible weapons and tools in your inventory and hotbar.
[*]Accessory Reforge Scroll (Goblin Tinkerer, 3 platinum by default): reforges eligible accessories across your inventory, loadouts, vanity slots, and modded accessory slots.
[*]Choose from the available modifiers, including modded ones, or use Best Available for weapons to select the modifier with the highest resulting item value. Non-whip summon weapons prefer Ruthless by default.
[*]Calamity's Horrible modifier is excluded by default. Can be disabled in the mod settings.
[*]Consumes one scroll only if at least one item changes, at no additional cost.
[*]No other mods needed.
[/list]
